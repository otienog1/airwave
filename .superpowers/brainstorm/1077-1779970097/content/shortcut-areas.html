Where should keyboard shortcuts focus?

Airwave has three main interactive areas — select all that apply

A

Player Controls

Space to play/pause · M to mute · arrow keys for volume · maybe next/prev station

B

Search & Filter

/ or Ctrl+K to focus search · Escape to clear · Tab through genre filters

C

Station Discovery

Arrow keys to navigate station grid · Enter to play · F to favorite

D

Global / App-wide

? to show shortcut cheatsheet · T to toggle theme · Escape to dismiss modals
